How the estimate is made
  • Your own wait grows by exactly one week per week, so your line on the chart above rises at a fixed slope. You are reached where it meets the queue's wait.
  • Speed is measured between real published updates inside each window, so a long gap between archived snapshots cannot masquerade as a burst of progress.
  • The central estimate uses the most recent window with enough updates behind it, because the long-run average includes a period when the queue behaved very differently.
  • The faster/slower scenarios are widened to a minimum spread — the honest uncertainty here is weeks, not days.
  • ISD state that they refresh the table every Monday, so a mid-week reading is normally unchanged rather than stalled. Source: ISD notice on renewal of registration, which also sets out that a card expiring while a renewal is queued does not by itself end your permission to work — read it there, not here.
